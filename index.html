The studioSlow photography

We shoot
slow.

Gaanix is a two-person studio working between Kigali and Lisbon. We build pictures the long way — conversations first, test frames, then the real thing.

Every commission ends with a physical print and a small digital edit. Nothing is batch-graded, nothing is left to a preset. If a frame needs six hours of light, we book the room for six hours.
